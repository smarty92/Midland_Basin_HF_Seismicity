This folder contains different datasets: cluster information, forecast results, and correlation analyses between well depth and "earthquake productivity", and between pore-pressure changes due to deep water disposal and the fraction of earthquakes related to hydraulic fracturing.

"clust_0_short.pkl", "clust_2_short.pkl", "clust_4_short.pkl", "clust_7_short.pkl", and "clust_9_short.pkl" contain the forecasts (in time and space) for the "short-term windows" presented in Figures 8 and 9 (Results). The notebook "Midland_Basin_HF_Seismicity/functions_and_notebooks/plot_forecast_results.ipynb" shows how to plot the results.

"clust_0_long.pkl", "clust_2_long.pkl", "clust_4_long.pkl", "clust_7_long.pkl", and "clust_9_long.pkl" contain the forecasts (in time and space) for the "long-term windows" presented in Figure 11 (Results). The notebook "Midland_Basin_HF_Seismicity/functions_and_notebooks/plot_forecast_results.ipynb" shows how to plot the results.

"all_clusters.pkl" contains the forecast results for the joint inversion (Figure 12).

"correlation_Hfdepth_EQ.pkl" and "correlation_SWD_Hf.pkl" contain the results of the correlation analyses between HF-related seismicity, deep water disposal-related pore-pressure changes, and HF well depth (Figure 13). The notebook "Midland_Basin_HF_Seismicity/functions_and_notebooks/figure13.ipynb" shows how to plot the analysis results.

"data_clusters.pkl" contains information on the 21 families identified as HF-related in our study. For each family, we provide information about the associated earthquakes and HF wells.
